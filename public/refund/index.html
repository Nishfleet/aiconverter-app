Refund Policy

The conversion flow is designed to be simple: preview first, pay only after seeing sample rows, then use the stronger redo if the full CSV is not good enough.

Free preview

If AI Converter cannot safely produce a sample preview, there is no charge. The file fails closed instead of being routed to a human queue.

Paid export retry

Paid jobs include one stronger automatic redo. Use it when the full CSV is incomplete, badly formatted, missing rows, or otherwise not usable. The redo uses the stored source PDF inside the short retention window.

Refund or credit

If a paid job still cannot produce a usable CSV after the stronger redo, AI Converter records refund or credit due. When automatic refunds are enabled and a cash refund is allowed, the refund is requested through the payment provider. Otherwise support completes the refund or credit review when the support workflow is available.

If a usable CSV has already been delivered and downloaded, support may offer credit instead of a cash refund, depending on the issue and abuse signals.

Anti-abuse limits

  • Repeated free previews from the same file or connection are limited.
  • Each paid job gets one automatic stronger redo.
  • Payment IDs are bound to one job and cannot be reused.
  • Refunds may be denied for altered files, duplicate claims, payment abuse, or attempts to bypass access controls.

How to request help

Contact support with your job ID, payment email, and a short description of what is wrong through the support form. Do not send bank statement PDFs through support; sensitive files should only go through the upload flow.
