How payment works

Pay after the preview, not before the guess.

Upload a file, inspect the preview, and download a sample CSV first. You are asked for payment only after the preview is available, so a file that cannot produce a reliable preview never reaches checkout. Paid jobs include one stronger automatic redo, and low-confidence conversions fail closed with no charge. Source files stay in private storage for the preview, unlock, and short redo window, and generated exports expire after a limited download window.

What each pack covers.

AI Converter sells one-time page packs in Indian Rupees. Starter covers 25 pages or images, Standard covers 100 pages or images, and Bulk covers 500 pages or images. The pack for a job is chosen from the estimated size of the file: up to 25 pages uses Starter, up to 100 pages uses Standard, and larger files use Bulk. There is no recurring subscription, and a file you only need converted once stays a single one-time purchase.

Every pack follows the same preview-first flow. Start from the converter, upload the file, and inspect the sample result before any payment is requested. The free sample CSV uses fictional data and shows the expected columns, dates, descriptions, totals, and row structure. If the preview looks wrong, stop there instead of paying for a full export - the flow is designed so a low-confidence or unusual file can fail closed before checkout.

For accounting workflows, compare the export with the source statement before importing it into bookkeeping, tax, lending, or compliance systems. AI Converter does not claim every bank, every statement format, guaranteed accuracy, or official integration with accounting platforms. Review the export against the original source, keep the original file until you have checked the result, and use the refund policy or support when a paid job needs review.
