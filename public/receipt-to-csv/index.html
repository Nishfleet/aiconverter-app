Limits

Use the preview as your quality gate.

AI Converter is built around a simple rule: inspect the sample before trusting the full export. That matters for receipts, bank statements, invoices, screenshots, and other sensitive files because automated extraction can be wrong when a scan is noisy, a receipt is crumpled or angled, a table is unusual, a file is damaged, or a provider cannot safely read the source. The preview gives you an early look at columns, dates, vendors, categories, totals, tax, and row structure before you pay for the complete result.

For expense workflows, compare the export with the original receipt before submitting it for reimbursement, reporting, or tax purposes. Keep the privacy pages close too: source files are handled privately and never used to train models, generated files have a short download window, and support requests should use job IDs rather than pasted receipt, bank, invoice, screenshot, or document contents.

A clean export is only useful when the route matches the file. Choose the receipt route for receipt photos and receipt PDFs, keep the original file until you have checked the result, and use the preview to spot missing vendors, wrong totals, missing tax, or categories that look off. If the preview looks wrong, stop there instead of paying for a full export.
