Use the preview as your quality gate.

AI Converter is built around a simple rule: inspect the sample before trusting the full export. That matters for receipts, bank statements, invoices, screenshots, and other sensitive files because automated extraction can be wrong when a photo is noisy, a receipt is unusual, a file is damaged, or a provider cannot safely read the source. The preview gives you an early look at vendor names, dates, totals, tax, currency, payment method, and row structure before you pay for the complete result.

For expense workflows, compare the export with the original receipt before sending it into bookkeeping, tax, reimbursement, or compliance systems. Keep the privacy pages close too: source files are handled for the job you start, generated files have a short download window, and support requests should use job IDs rather than pasted receipt, bank, invoice, screenshot, or document contents.

A clean export is only useful when the route matches the file. Upload the clearest photo or PDF you have, keep the original until you have checked the result, and use the row preview to spot missing dates, wrong vendors, weak OCR, or totals and tax that do not match the receipt. If the preview looks off, stop there instead of paying for a full export.
