What the CSV contains

Expense CSV, one row per readable page.

Each row carries date, vendor, category, total, currency, subtotal, tax, payment method, and notes when each field is safely detected. Vendor detection, tax and total checks, and category inference run before an export is allowed.

Supported inputs

PDF, PNG, JPG, JPEG, or WEBP.

Files up to 50 MB are accepted. PDFs may have up to 500 pages; larger files should be split before uploading. Image and PDF signatures are validated before processing, so mislabeled files are rejected early.

Limits

No vendor and total, no export.

Reading receipts is OCR-sensitive: blurry photos, glare, cut-off edges, folded or handwritten receipts, and unusual layouts can fail. If a vendor and total cannot be found, or the trust score is too low, the converter refuses instead of guessing. Corrupted or password-protected files may also fail.

Use the preview as your quality gate.

AI Converter is built around a simple rule: inspect the sample before trusting the full export. That matters for receipts, bank statements, invoices, screenshots, and other sensitive files because automated extraction can be wrong when a photo is noisy, a receipt is folded, a file is damaged, or the text cannot be read safely. The preview gives you an early look at dates, vendors, totals, and row structure — with a confidence score — before you pay for the complete result.

A multi-page PDF is treated as one row per readable receipt page, and a single page is merged into one row, so the CSV should match the number of receipts you expect. Compare the exported rows with the paper receipt before importing them into expense reports, bookkeeping, tax, or compliance systems. Keep the original file until you have checked the result, and use the sample rows to spot missing totals, wrong vendors, or dates that do not match.

If a receipt cannot be structured safely, the preview fails closed with no charge and the source file is deleted. Source files are handled privately for the job you start: they are kept for the preview, unlock, and redo window, generated files have a short download window, and support requests should use job IDs rather than pasted receipt or document contents.
