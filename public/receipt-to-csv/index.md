# Receipt to expense CSV

AI Converter turns receipt photos and PDFs into expense CSV rows you can review. Upload the receipt, check sample rows first, then unlock the full CSV only when the preview looks useful.

## Workflow

1. Upload a receipt PDF, PNG, JPG, JPEG, or WEBP up to 50 MB.
2. Review sample rows before payment.
3. Unlock the full CSV if the preview is usable.
4. Download the generated CSV.

## Output

Typical columns are date, vendor, category, total, currency, subtotal, tax, payment method, and notes when safely detected. Beta extraction can produce one row per readable receipt page.

## Limits

Files can fail when they are low quality, blurry, folded or cut off, unusual, corrupted, or unsupported. When OCR confidence is too low or a receipt cannot be safely structured, the job fails closed with no charge. AI Converter does not claim every receipt will work.

No email intake is requested. The export should be reviewed before important use.
