# Receipt to expense CSV

AI Converter turns receipt photos and PDFs into expense CSV rows. Upload the receipt, check the extracted sample rows first, then unlock the full CSV only when the preview looks useful. No receipt inbox, no manual data-entry queue, and no card or bank login is requested.

## Workflow

1. Upload a receipt PDF, PNG, JPG, or WEBP up to 50 MB.
2. Review the extracted sample rows before payment.
3. Unlock the expense CSV if the preview is usable.
4. Download the rows and compare them with the original receipt.

## Output

The expense CSV uses date, vendor, category, total, currency, subtotal, tax, payment method, and notes when those fields can be safely detected. Category is inferred and should be treated as a suggestion. Fields can be missing when the receipt is not clear enough to read.

## Limits

This is a beta route. Blurry photos, cut-off text, handwriting, unusual layouts, password-protected, corrupted, or unsupported files may fail. When the converter cannot safely find a vendor and total, or trust is too low, the job fails closed and you are not charged. AI Converter does not claim every receipt works.

The export should be reviewed against the original receipt before expense reports, reimbursements, or tax use. Support requests should reference job IDs, not pasted receipt contents.
