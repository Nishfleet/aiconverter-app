Xero CSV prep

Convert bank statement PDFs into CSV rows for Xero review.

AI Converter extracts reviewable rows from a bank statement PDF so you can prepare a CSV for Xero. The export still needs checking, cleanup, and mapping.

No direct bank feed. No Xero account connection.

Use case

When the bank only gives you a PDF.

Use this page when you have statement pages and need rows you can inspect before preparing a Xero import file.

Boundaries

Xero import is your review step.

AI Converter does not connect to Xero or guarantee that Xero will accept the file without edits. Review dates, amounts, signs, and descriptions before import.

Workflow

Check rows before paying.

  • Upload a PDF statement.
  • Review the free sample preview.
  • Unlock the full CSV when the preview is usable.
  • Clean and map the CSV in your Xero workflow.

Data handling

Short retention.

Source files are stored privately for preview, unlock, and redo, then deleted after failed extraction, completed redo, or the 24-hour source lifecycle.
