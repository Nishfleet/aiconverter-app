About

A focused converter for sensitive files.

AI Converter converts bank statement PDFs to CSV through direct upload, free sample preview, paid full extraction, and download. Receipt and screenshot-table converters are beta modules on the same workflow.

What is live now

  • Bank statement PDF to CSV conversion.
  • Free sample preview before payment.
  • Paid full CSV unlock from $3.
  • Parser-first extraction for digital PDFs.
  • OCR fallback for scanned or messy PDFs when configured.
  • Low-confidence files fail closed.

What is beta

Receipt image or PDF to expense CSV and screenshot table to spreadsheet CSV are beta. Receipts can include category, tax, payment method, and notes when safely detected. Screenshot tables can infer headers when safe. Both use OCR and can fail closed when the extracted structure is not reliable.

What we avoid claiming

AI Converter does not claim support for every bank, every statement format, every receipt format, every screenshot layout, every file type, certified compliance, or guaranteed accuracy. Review every export before important use.
