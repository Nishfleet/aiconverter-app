About

A focused converter for sensitive files.

AI Converter converts bank statement PDFs to CSV through direct upload, free sample preview, paid full extraction, and download. Receipt, invoice, screenshot-table, audio transcript, document Markdown, and screenshot-to-HTML converters are beta modules on the same workflow. PNG/JPG/WEBP image and SVG conversion runs locally in the browser.

What is live now

  • Bank statement PDF to CSV conversion.
  • Free sample preview before payment.
  • Paid full CSV unlock from $3.
  • Parser-first extraction for digital PDFs.
  • OCR fallback for scanned or messy PDFs when configured.
  • Low-confidence files fail closed.
  • PNG/JPG/WEBP to PNG/JPG/WEBP browser-local conversion.
  • PNG/JPG/WEBP to SVG browser-local posterized conversion.

What is beta

Receipt image or PDF to expense CSV, invoice or bill image/PDF to CSV or JSON, screenshot table to spreadsheet CSV, audio transcript, document Markdown, and screenshot-to-HTML starter are beta. They use configured AI providers and can fail closed when the extracted content is not reliable.

What we avoid claiming

AI Converter does not claim support for every bank, every statement format, every receipt format, every invoice format, every screenshot layout, every audio file, every document, every image format, every file type, certified compliance, pixel-perfect screenshot-to-code, or guaranteed accuracy. Review every export before important use.
