Scanned statement OCR

Turn scanned bank statements into rows you can open in Excel.

For image-heavy PDFs, AI Converter can use OCR fallback when configured. The result is a CSV that opens in Excel, not a guaranteed native Excel workbook.

Low-confidence scans should fail closed instead of inventing rows.

What you get

Excel-readable CSV.

The export is CSV rows you can open with Excel or another spreadsheet tool. Review the rows, amounts, and balances before using the file.

OCR fallback

Useful for scans, not magic.

OCR can help with scanned or photo-based PDFs, but blurry pages, shadows, cut-off columns, and handwriting can still fail or require cleanup.

Workflow

Preview the scan result first.

  • Upload the scanned PDF.
  • Check whether the preview rows match the statement.
  • Unlock the full CSV only when the preview is useful.
  • Open the CSV in Excel and review before important use.

Limits

Quality matters.

Clean scans with visible columns work best. Password-protected, corrupted, very low-resolution, or unusual files may fail.
