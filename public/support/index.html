Support

Get help

For conversion, payment, refund, deletion, or security requests, use this support form. Include the job ID when you have one. Do not send full bank statement, receipt, invoice, screenshot, or source-file details through support.

Send a request

What to include

  • Job ID and payment email, if available.
  • The plan you selected.
  • A short description of what went wrong.
  • Whether you already tried the stronger redo.

Do not send source files

Sensitive files should only be submitted through the upload flow. Email monitoring is planned as a later feature, but the current support form should not receive sensitive files or copied source data.

Refund and redo help

Paid jobs include one automatic stronger redo. If the redo still cannot produce a usable CSV or JSON file, the job is marked refund or credit due. Support can use the recorded job status and payment record when reviewing the request.

Deletion requests

Source files are deleted automatically after the 24-hour private source lifecycle or earlier after failed extraction or completed redo. For a deletion request, send the job ID and payment email only through the form.

Expected response

Support requests are recorded for review. Security reports and payment, deletion, or refund issues are treated as priority requests.
