Privacy Policy

AI Converter handles sensitive source files only for the conversion workflow you start. The service is built around private upload, short retention, minimal metadata, and no human review queue.

Information we collect

  • The source file you upload for conversion.
  • Generated preview rows and CSV or JSON output.
  • Optional email address for payment receipt and job recovery.
  • Payment status, payment ID, selected plan, timestamps, and job status.
  • Security metadata such as hashed IP, hashed user agent, file hash, and abuse-limit events.

We do not ask for bank login credentials and you should never provide them.

How files are processed

Digital bank statement PDFs are parsed directly first. If a file is scanned, image-heavy, a receipt, an invoice, a screenshot, or too messy for the native parser, Mistral OCR may be used when configured. Azure Document Intelligence may be used as an optional paid-job fallback when configured. Provider use is limited to extracting transaction data for the conversion you requested.

Low-confidence files fail closed instead of being sent to a human review queue. If the sample preview cannot be produced safely, there is no charge.

PNG/JPG/WEBP image-format conversion runs locally in the browser and does not upload the image to AI Converter for that route.

Payments

Payments are handled by the payment provider. AI Converter stores payment status and payment identifiers needed to unlock a job, prevent payment reuse, and process refunds or credits. We do not store full card numbers.

Retention

Source files are kept only for preview, paid unlock, and the automatic redo window. Source files are deleted after a failed preview, failed full extraction, completed redo, or the 24-hour private source lifecycle. Generated CSV and JSON files expire after 7 days. Browser-local image conversions do not create source files or generated files on AI Converter.

Minimal job metadata may be kept longer for payment records, abuse prevention, service debugging, and legal or accounting obligations.

Security and abuse prevention

Uploaded files are stored privately, job access uses random tokens, and API responses are served with no-store caching. The service limits repeated free previews, repeated same-file uploads, payment reuse, and repeated redo attempts to reduce abuse.

Your requests

For deletion, privacy, or payment-related requests, use the support form. Do not send source files through support; use the upload flow only.
